# Changelog

## 0.8.0 — Sound and polish

- **Sound.** Footsteps that change with what is underfoot, the thud of the hoe, water, a coin, a door, a bite and a catch, a lullaby when the day ends, thunder, a chime when you discover a creature, a fanfare for a prize; a soft background that follows the weather, the season, the hour and the place (breeze, rain, a storm, snow's hush, crickets, water, a hum indoors, a cave's drone); and a slow tune for each season that plays now and then. All of it is made as you play, with nothing downloaded or stored. It starts after your first key press, stops when the window is not in front, and has volume sliders and a mute in the pause menu.
- **Comfort.** Text size (normal or large), a high-contrast look, and a motion choice (follow your computer, calm, or full) in the pause menu. The long menu scrolls in a small window.
- **Fairer numbers.** Ore, coal and crystals sell for about 40% less, fish for a fifth to a quarter less (and bite five times a day, not six), pumpkins sell for more (autumn was the poor season). The economy is now a test that keeps farming, fishing and mining in step.
- **Under the hood.** The game's long main file was split into parts for the weather and festivals and for the creatures; performance is guarded by a test. Saves are unchanged: farms saved with 0.7.0 open as they were, and preferences from older versions are read with the new options at their defaults.

- **Listing and licence.** The game is MIT-licensed (© 2026 Edes Sulce), with the licences of the bundled components in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The Marketplace listing now shows the farmhouse icon, and the editor tab carries a coloured farmhouse icon: the old line-art glyph used `currentColor`, which a tab cannot resolve, so it was invisible on dark themes.

Not in yet: rebinding keys, new character animation, and anything beyond the valley's seven regions of the roadmap. The sound has not been tuned by ear.

## 0.7.0 — Beyond the farm

- **Two new places.** The fallen logs on the farm's north and south trails are gone. North is **Windcrest Heights**: a windswept highland with a tarn, ore, standing stones, and an old ruin with an arch (open once you have dug up three buried things). South is **Marlow Marsh**: shallows with boardwalks, a fen, and Fern's lodge on stilts (open once you have caught your first fish for Hollis and shown something to the museum). Both have dig spots, fishing and plenty of room to wander.
- **Rare creatures.** A fen heron, a kingfisher, a moon moth, a mountain hare, a ruin owl and a white stag, each about only in its own weather, season and hours. Walk up to one quietly (do not run) to see it, give a seen one something it likes to befriend it, and it may leave you a keepsake. The journal has a new **Creatures** page.
- **Fern**, the naturalist: a shelf with moonbloom seeds and fertilizer, and three quests about the creatures and the flower that only grows in the rain.
- **Magical farming.** *Fertilizer* makes a good harvest far likelier. *Sprinklers* water the soil around them every night. *Moonbloom* grows only on nights it rains.
- **Fixes found by a review of this version.** Moonbloom no longer grows under glass; ore on Windcrest comes back every morning; creatures stay where they were after you mine a rock and reopen the game; quests count creatures you had already met; the Creatures page updates at once; the white stag walks the heights in autumn rain as well as storms.
- **Fixes from a review of 0.6.** A festival prize can be collected any time until bedtime (and one you forget is brought round in the night); the closed stall says which day it opens next; lanterns wait for whoever is standing where they hang; the rival scores and bonuses are in the festival data; eggs and creatures are never hidden behind a tree.
- **Saves** now keep the creatures you have met and which soil is fertilized. Farms saved with 0.6.0 open as they were.

Not in yet: audio, polish, and balance (Phase 8).

## 0.6.0 — A living world

- **Weather.** Every day has its sky: sun, cloud, rain, storm or (in winter) snow, the same for everyone on a given day and never random on reload. The clock panel shows today and tomorrow.
  Rain and storms water every outdoor field overnight (a greenhouse is never rained on), the fish bite a little longer, the light dims, and rain, storms (with the odd flash) and snow are drawn.
- **The seasons look different.** Spring is fresh, summer warm, autumn amber and winter cool, and the oaks turn in autumn.
- **Four festivals.** The Egg Hunt (spring 13), the Silverbend Fishing Contest (summer 11), the Harvest Fair (autumn 16) and Lantern Night (winter 25). The villagers gather, the host explains, and you
  are ranked against three rivals for a prize you collect once (ask any time until bedtime: one you forget is brought round in the night). Lantern Night hangs lanterns on the green.
- **A notice board** on the village green with three requests a day from the villagers, paid in gold (more from a friend) and goodwill.
- **Market day.** On Sundays Rowan, a travelling merchant, sells every kind of seed, whatever the season, from a cart on the green. Seeds out of season are for your greenhouse.
- **A calendar** in the journal: the season in four weeks with festivals, birthdays and market days marked, and what is coming up.
- **People have days.** Tobias stays in on rainy days and Bram works in the store; a shop is never closed by the weather alone.
- **Fixes from a review of 0.5.** The rug can be put down (and furniture can stand on it); dig spots are the same after a reload; quests count what you did before taking them up; furniture is never
  put down on you; and the museum remembers its rewards by the count that earned them.
- **Saves** now keep today's festival progress and requests. Farms saved with 0.5.0 open as they were (the museum's paid rewards are carried over).

Not in yet: events beyond the market, snow on the ground, banners and stalls at the other festivals, the remaining regions, audio.

## 0.5.0 — Making a home

- **The farmhouse has an inside.** Walk into the door: a bed, a kitchen and a workbench. The bed is how you end the day.
- **Cook and make things.** Thirteen recipes: fry an egg, make a salad, a soup, jam or a pie (some recipes are taught by friends), smelt ore into
  copper and iron bars, and make lanterns, vases and a grandfather clock. Dishes sell for more than what went into them.
- **Furniture.** Buy chairs, tables, shelves, rugs and more from Hollis, put them down in your farmhouse with the use key and pick them
  up again with the interact key. They are saved.
- **A carpenter and a greenhouse.** Bram builds a greenhouse on the empty lot east of your house: money, stone and copper bars, three nights
  to build. Under glass, crops grow in every season and never wither.
- **Dig up the past.** A few mounds of earth appear every morning on the farm, the river bank and in the wood: swing the hoe at one and see
  what is buried. Take what you find to the new **museum** on the green, where Ines pays rewards as the cases fill.
- **Five new quests** and two new villagers (Bram and Ines).
- **Fairer fishing and mining.** Each water gives only a few bites a day, a reward is never half given or lost to full pockets, and broken rocks
  and dug spots stay used up for the day even if you close the game.
- **Saves** now keep furniture, buildings, the museum and what has been used up today. Farms saved with 0.4.0 open as they were.

Not in yet: weather, festivals, the remaining regions, audio.

## 0.4.0 — The wider valley

- **Go inside.** Walk into the door of Willa's cottage, Tobias's cottage or the new General Store. Villagers go home for the night
  through their own doors, and Hollis keeps the shop from nine until five.
- **Fishing.** Buy a rod at the store and cast at the farm's river and pond, at Silverbend below the village, or at the pond in
  Whispering Wood. Wait for the float to dip, hook it, and keep the marker in the green. Six fish bite by water, season and hour, and
  none of it can ever cost you anything.
- **The old mine.** Past the pond in Whispering Wood, a cave mouth leads down ten floors of rock. Buy a pickaxe, break stone, copper, coal,
  iron, quartz and amethyst (deeper floors have the better ones), and take the ladders up and down. Every rock is back by morning.
- **New people.** Hollis Reed, who keeps the store (and buys fish, ore and produce), and June Hale, the angler of Silverbend.
- **Four more quests**, and the journal now has a **Collection** page for the fish you have caught and the minerals you have dug up.
- **The way into the wood is shut** until you have helped Hollis. A closed way tells you why.
- **Saves** now keep what you have found and how deep you have been. Farms saved with 0.3.0 open as they were.

Not in yet: crafting, cooking, building, a museum, weather, festivals, audio.

## 0.3.0 — A living world

- **People.** Willa, the seed seller, and Tobias, a retired miller, have homes, birthdays, tastes and a day to live: they walk
  between the Village Green and the farm along the road and over the bridge. Talk to them (`E`), chat once a day, and give them
  gifts they like. They remember, and at some hearts they give you things.
- **The Village Green.** Walk off the east end of the farm road to arrive on a second map: two cottages, a fountain, benches.
  The farm is kept exactly as you left it.
- **A real stall.** Willa minds the seed stall from about half past eight until five. She sells seeds by the season, chicken
  feed and chickens, and buys what you grew at a price that changes from day to day.
- **Hens.** A coop with a feed trough on the farm. Feed the hens, and each fed hen leaves an egg overnight; pet them, collect the
  eggs, get to know them.
- **Quests and a journal.** Four small quests from the villagers; press `J` to see them, their progress and their rewards. The
  morning report mentions eggs and anything ready to report.
- **Saves** now keep friendships, hens, quests and story flags. Farms saved with 0.2.0 open exactly as they were, with everyone
  meeting you for the first time.
- Villagers are solid: you cannot walk through them.

Not in yet: fishing, mining, crafting, building, weather, audio, interiors, the rest of the village.

## 0.2.0 — The first playable farm

- **A day to work in.** A clock from 6:00 am to 2:00 am, a four-season calendar, a soft day-night tint, going to bed at the
  farmhouse door (or falling asleep at 2:00 am), and a morning report.
- **Farm.** Hoe the fenced field, plant seeds, water with the can, sleep, and pick what grew. Five crops (radish, strawberry,
  tomato, sunflower, pumpkin), two of which fruit again. Crops grow only on watered days and wither when their season ends.
  Some harvests come out better and sell for more.
- **Pockets.** A twelve-slot hotbar with icons, counts and a water meter. A green or red cursor shows what an item will do.
- **Selling and buying.** Ship produce in the bin by the house and be paid in the morning; buy seeds at the stall.
- **Saves** now keep the clock, pockets, money and the whole farm. Farms saved with 0.1.0 open as a new farm with the farmer where
  they were.
- Controls: `Space` or a click uses the item; `E` looks and picks.

Not in yet: animals, villagers, fishing, mining, crafting, building, weather, audio.

## 0.1.0 — Foundation

The first playable build of the world itself.

- Home Farm, a 64 × 48 cell map: farmhouse, well, mailbox, signs, a fenced field, a wooden bridge, a river and a pond, groves, and
  four trails that are closed for now.
- Walk and run with smooth collision; walk behind trees and the farmhouse roof; read signs and look at the door.
- Animated water, chimney smoke, soft shadows; crisp pixels at any zoom and display density.
- A pixel-art interface in forest green, wood and parchment: location banner, prompts, message box, how-to-play hints, pause menu.
- Automatic and manual saving, with a backup, recovery from a damaged save, and protection for saves from newer versions.
- Fully offline. No account, no telemetry.

Not in yet: farming, animals, villagers, fishing, mining, crafting, building, weather, seasons, audio.
